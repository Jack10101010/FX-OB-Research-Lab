## Architecture notes

- **1-min execution**: `DEFAULT_CANDLE_FILE = "EURUSD_1m.csv"`. `simulate_trades()` receives 1-min candles directly.
- **15-min detection**: `resample_candles()` used for OB detection only; execution always 1-min.
- **`TRADE_COLUMNS` hard filter**: `pd.DataFrame(trades, columns=TRADE_COLUMNS)` at execution.py line ~1975. Any field not in this list is silently dropped. Always add new fields to both `TRADE_COLUMNS` and `_base_trade_row()`.
- **`BacktestConfig` frozen dataclass**: use `replace(ACTIVE_CONFIG, **overrides)` pattern. New fields with defaults flow through automatically.
- **Delay semantics**: arm records immediately on first detection; `_be_stop_active_from = arm_candle_index + delay_candles` gates the stop check. With `delay=0`, stop active on arm candle itself.
- **Same-candle arm+stop**: conservative — BE fires (matches `_exit_outcome` stop-wins rule).
